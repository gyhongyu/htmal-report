Supply Chain Logistics Logic

  • Hub-and-Spoke Setup: Dispatches finished PCBA modules directly to client appliance assembly units.
  • Hub-and-Spoke 倉儲： 完成之 PCBA 模組直送 OEM 客戶家電廠。
