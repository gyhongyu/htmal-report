Glossary of Terms & Abbreviations

Term Full Name Description & Scope
DRI Direct Responsible Individual The single person accountable for a specific module's execution and audit deliverables.
FACA Failure Analysis & Corrective Action Process of analyzing root causes of defects and implementing systemic corrective actions.
SMT Surface Mount Technology Automated electronics manufacturing process including solder printing, placement, reflow, and depaneling.
AOI Automated Optical Inspection Visual inspection equipment scanning printed circuit board assemblies for soldering and component defects.
IQC / OQA Incoming Quality Control / Outgoing QA Quality verification controls applied to incoming raw materials and finished goods before shipment.
ESD Electrostatic Discharge Control Static electricity prevention protocols across manufacturing lines to protect delicate electronic components.
FW Firmware Embedded software installed on hardware components subject to strict revision control and rework auditing.
QS Quality System Comprehensive organizational framework and compliance documentation governing audit procedures.
PM Project Management Overall project coordination, milestone tracking, and cross-departmental scheduling.
POC Point of Contact Primary designated liaison handling communication between auditee FOXLINK and client auditor SSSTC.
FTP File Transfer Protocol Secure digital repository server used for uploading official audit documentation and evidence files.
