Supply Chain Gaps & FOXLINK Opportunity

Localization Gap: Seeking vendors with precision stamping, modular connectors, TIMs, and "Make in India" compliance.

在地化需求： 尋求精密金屬沖壓、模組化連接器、導熱介質 (TIM) 及符合 "Make in India" 的合作夥伴。

FOXLINK Pitch: Inceram handles ceramic sintering but lacks wire harness, metal stamping & connector packaging. Foxlink can offer a turnkey "Ceramic + Metal + Connector" module to tap into global OEM electronics supply chains.

FOXLINK 綜效： Inceram 具備陶瓷燒結，但缺乏線束與連接器整合。Foxlink 可提供「陶瓷+金屬+連接器」模組化組裝方案，共同切入國際 OEM 供應鏈。
